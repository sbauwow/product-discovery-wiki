# Product Discovery Wiki Backlog

Prioritized gaps to add next. Ordered by leverage, not by theoretical completeness.

## Tier 1 — add first

### 1. ICP, Segmentation, and Beachhead Market
Why:
- The wiki covers personas, JTBD, PMF, pricing, and strategy, but not the practical question of who to target first.
- This is the missing bridge between abstract strategy and concrete discovery.

Should cover:
- segment vs persona vs ICP
- firmographic / behavioral / needs-based segmentation
- beachhead selection
- who not to serve
- signs of bad segmentation

Status:
- Added: `concepts/icp-segmentation.md`

### 2. Competitive Analysis and Positioning
Why:
- Strong macro-strategy coverage, weak practical differentiation coverage.
- Discovery needs a disciplined view of alternatives, not just user pain.

Should cover:
- direct vs indirect competitors
- alternatives mapping
- positioning statement
- strengths / weaknesses / switching costs
- how to avoid feature-comparison traps

Status:
- Added: `concepts/competitive-analysis-positioning.md`

### 3. Research Synthesis
Why:
- The wiki explains how to collect evidence but not how to turn it into decisions.
- This is where many teams fail.

Should cover:
- affinity mapping
- clustering patterns
- insight writing
- evidence strength / triangulation
- turning observations into opportunities

Status:
- Added: `concepts/research-synthesis.md`

### 4. Experiment Design
Why:
- RAT and A/B pages exist, but the practical design of a good test is missing.

Should cover:
- hypothesis format
- success criteria
- guardrail metrics
- stopping rules
- false positives / novelty effects
- test ladder from cheap to expensive

Status:
- Added: `concepts/experiment-design.md`

### 5. Tracking Plans and Instrumentation
Why:
- Funnel, cohort, activation, HEART, and A/B all depend on clean telemetry.
- Missing instrumentation guidance makes the quant side incomplete.

Should cover:
- event taxonomy
- naming conventions
- property design
- tracking plans
- instrumentation QA
- metric drift / data trust

Status:
- Added: `concepts/tracking-plans-instrumentation.md`
